## Welcome to Latrocore

**Latrocore** is your personal diabetes companion and digital care portal. Designed in collaboration with diabetologists and clinical pharmacists, Latrocore is created to take the guesswork, anxiety, and confusion out of managing diabetes.

Whether you are tracking daily blood sugars, ensuring tablets are taken on time with live sound alarms, planning healthy low-glycemic meals, or preparing for your doctor's visit, this guide explains what each feature does, why it matters to your health, and how to use it step-by-step.

## 1. Patient Dashboard & Daily Vitals Overview

### What It Is
The **Dashboard** is the first screen you see when you sign in. It acts as your command center, giving you an immediate high-level summary of your health for today.

### Purpose & Health Benefit
Managing diabetes requires keeping an eye on four essential numbers. Instead of digging through lab slips, these 4 summary cards show you exactly where you stand right now:
- **Latest Glucose**: Shows your most recent blood sugar reading (e.g. 110 mg/dL), what time you took it, and whether it was taken fasting or after food.
- **Latest HbA1c**: Displays your 3-month average blood sugar level (e.g. 7.2%). Knowing this helps you understand whether your current medication and diet are keeping you in the safe target zone.
- **Medication Adherence**: A percentage score (e.g. 97%) showing how reliably you have taken your scheduled tablets on time over the past 30 days. Keeping adherence above 80% is the single best way to prevent complications.
- **Activity Progression**: Tracks how many minutes of physical exercise you have completed today against your doctor-recommended daily goal of 30 minutes.

### How to Use It
- Simply glance at the 4 cards when you open the app each morning.
- Click any of the cards to jump directly into the full history or detailed logging screen.

4. **Ask Assistant**: Open an instant chat with your friendly diabetes AI bot for meal or health advice.

6. **View Prescriptions**: Review your official doctor's prescription and dosage timings.

## 3. Tablet Scheduler & Audible Alarm Engine

### What It Is
A smart, interactive medication clock that sits on your dashboard to guide you through your exact medicine routine throughout the day.

### Purpose & Health Benefit
Missing doses or taking tablets at the wrong time (such as taking Metformin on an empty stomach) can lead to stomach upset or sudden sugar spikes. 

The **Tablet Scheduler** ensures you never miss a dose:
- **Real-Time Sound Alarm**: When it is time to take your tablet (e.g. 08:30 AM), your phone or computer plays a gentle chime and speaks out: *"Reminder: Time to take your tablet Metformin HCl"*.
- **Food Instructions**: Clearly states whether each tablet must be taken before meals, with food, or after dinner.
- **Audible On/Off Toggle**: You can turn the sound alarm ON or Muted with a single button if you are in a meeting or sleeping.

### How to Use It
1. When your alarm rings, click the green **"Take & Stop"** button. This automatically logs your dose into your clinical history, increases your adherence score, and dismisses the alarm chime.
2. If you took your tablet without waiting for the alarm, simply click the **"Taken"** button next to the medicine in the list.

## 4. Diabetes Test Reminder Schedule

### What It Is
An automated, structured testing schedule based on the **American Diabetes Association (ADA 2026)** clinical guidelines, accessible both from your dashboard and as a full dedicated page (`/patient/test-reminders`).

### Purpose & Health Benefit
Diabetes is not just about daily finger-prick checks; protecting your heart, eyes, and kidneys requires routine laboratory tests at specific intervals. The schedule organizes your care into three clear tiers:

#### Tier 1: Every 3 Months (HbA1c & Glycemic Review)
- **Why It Matters**: Red blood cells live for about 90 to 120 days. Testing your HbA1c every 3 months gives an exact report card of your average control.
- **Included**: HbA1c test, fasting/post-meal review, and medication side-effect check.

#### Tier 2: Every 6 Months (Health & Laboratory Checkpoint)
- **Why It Matters**: Evaluates how your body is handling medications and monitors cholesterol.
- **Included**: Lipid profile (good and bad cholesterol), liver function checks, and blood pressure review.

#### Tier 3: At Least Annually (Complication & Preventive Health Review)
- **Why It Matters**: Microvascular damage to eyes, kidneys, and nerves develops silently without symptoms. Annual screening detects changes years before symptoms appear.
- **Included**: Dilated retinal eye photography, urine microalbumin (kidney test), and comprehensive foot nerve testing.

### Patient Welfare Workflow Protocol
Underneath your schedule tiers is the **Patient Welfare Banner**:
$$\text{Remind} \longrightarrow \text{Record} \longrightarrow \text{Complete} \longrightarrow \text{Escalate}$$
- **Remind**: The app automatically notifies you 14 days before a test is due.
- **Record**: Your clinic reports are securely stored in your history.
- **Complete**: The system compares your lab results against your personalized targets.
- **Escalate**: If any value is out-of-range, your doctor is instantly flagged to adjust your care.

## 5. Diabetic Diet Plan & Macro Budget

### What It Is
A personalized, meal-by-meal dietary guide tailored specifically for diabetic glycemic control, with culturally relevant meal options (including Indian diets like vegetable ragi idlis, dal, and multigrain rotis).

### Purpose & Health Benefit
Food directly affects blood sugar. Eating the right balance of complex carbohydrates, lean protein, and healthy dietary fiber prevents dangerous post-meal glucose spikes.

### Key Features
- **Daily Macro Budget**: Displays your daily limits at a glance:
  - Carbohydrates: $< 130\text{g}$ total for the day
  - Protein: $75\text{g}$ daily target
  - Hydration: $3.0\text{L}$ water tracker
- **Meal-by-Meal Timeline**: Interactive tabs for **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Evening Snack**, and **Dinner**.
- **Clinical Nutrition Tips**: Practical guidance for each meal (e.g., *"Eat your raw salad and fiber first before rice or roti to slow down carbohydrate absorption"*).

## 7. Glucose Monitor & 30-Day Trend Charts

### What It Is
An interactive logbook and continuous trend analyzer for all your glucometer and CGM (Continuous Glucose Monitor) readings.

### Purpose & Health Benefit
A single blood sugar reading is just a snapshot; what matters is the trend over days and weeks.
- **Interactive Glycemic Curve**: Graphically visualizes your blood sugars over 7 days or 30 days.
- **Safe Target Zone Boundaries**: Shaded green bands show the normal fasting target ($80 - 130\text{ mg/dL}$). Readings above or below this corridor are clearly highlighted in amber or red.
- **Context Tagging**: Each reading is tagged by meal timing (Fasting, 2 Hours After Lunch, Bedtime, Post-Dinner). This helps your doctor determine whether a spike was caused by breakfast carbs or missed night insulin.
- **CSV Data Export**: Easily export your complete readings as a clean table to share with family or your clinic.

## 9. Clinical Care Checklist (8 Essential Health Checks)

### What It Is
An 8-point checklist based on ADA Standards of Care, displayed as a compact overview on your dashboard and accessible as a full dedicated page (`/patient/checklist`).

### Purpose & Health Benefit
Rather than worrying whether you forgot a health checkup, this page provides 8 clean cards that track your annual care:
1. **Medication adherence $\ge 80\%$** *(Daily therapy adherence)*
2. **Glucose logged today** *(Daily finger-stick or sensor check)*
3. **HbA1c recorded this quarter** *(3-month laboratory evaluation)*
4. **Blood pressure recorded this month** *(Target $< 130/80\text{ mmHg}$)*
5. **Eye screening current** *(Annual dilated fundus exam for retinopathy)*
6. **Foot examination current** *(Annual sensory test to prevent ulcers)*
7. **Kidney monitoring current** *(Annual urine microalbumin & eGFR)*
8. **30+ minutes activity this week** *(Weekly metabolic fitness goal)*

### How to Use It
- Check the completion score on your dashboard (e.g. 50% Complete).
- Tap **"View All 8 Cards"** to open the dedicated page.
- Use the filter tabs (**All**, **Completed**, **Action Needed**) to see exactly which appointments or logs you need to complete next.

## 11. Preventive Screenings

### What It Is
A surveillance schedule designed to monitor the three organs most vulnerable to long-term diabetes: **Eyes**, **Kidneys**, and **Feet**.

### Purpose & Health Benefit
- **Retinal Surveillance**: High blood sugar can silently weaken tiny blood vessels in the retina. Regular photography catches changes before they affect vision.
- **Kidney Assessment**: Checks urine for microalbumin (tiny protein leaks), the earliest indicator of kidney strain.
- **Sensory Foot Exam**: Uses a gentle monofilament test to check whether nerve sensations in the toes and soles remain completely healthy.

## 12. Safety Alerts & Hypoglycemia Warning System

### What It Is
An automated clinical safety guardian that monitors your readings and triggers immediate alerts when urgent action is needed.

### Purpose & Health Benefit
- **Hypoglycemia Alert ($< 70\text{ mg/dL}$)**: Low blood sugar requires immediate treatment to avoid dizziness or fainting.
- **The "Rule of 15" Protocol**:
  1. Take $15\text{g}$ of fast-acting sugar (3 glucose tablets, 1/2 cup fruit juice, or 1 tablespoon honey).
  2. Rest for 15 minutes.
  3. Re-check blood sugar.
  4. Repeat until glucose is safely above $70\text{ mg/dL}$.
- **Automatic Care Team Notification**: If severe readings recur, the app alerts your doctor for medication adjustment.
